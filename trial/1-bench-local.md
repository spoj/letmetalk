- **Warm setup** dropped with the DCEP round trip, which a stream on a live link no longer pays. On loopback, the first
  echoed byte takes about one RTT, so the warm criterion (one RTT plus 10 ms) holds with room.
- **Cold setup** fell from 606 to 59 ms for native↔native. I did not isolate the cause.
- **Throughput:** native↔native is 2–11% up (6% and 11% for one stream, 2% for four), with ranges that no longer overlap. The browser pairs' medians
  fell, but their ranges overlap the old ones: Chromium↔Chromium uploads ran 24.7–96.4 MB/s, against 69.1–96.2 before.
  This run does not settle that. Even the lowest median, 37.8 MB/s, is about 300 Mbit/s, above the 50 Mbit/s criterion.
- **Latency under load:** native↔native's p50 rose, from 2.42 to 3.69 ms, while p95 and p99 fell. All of them are far
  under the criterion of one RTT plus 100 ms.
- The machine was shared: the load average at the start of each run was 2.3 to 6.2.
